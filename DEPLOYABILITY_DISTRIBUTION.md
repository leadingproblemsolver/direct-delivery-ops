# Deployability and distribution

## Deployment unit

A local/CI validation CLI and run-directory contract. Browser-use or other acquisition agents may populate the workspace, but cannot bypass its source, selection, artifact, or approval gates.

## Distribution channel

The produced unit is a source-linked Context Recovery Pack delivered manually after human approval. The repository itself is distributed as an operator tool, not as a broad end-user platform.

## Release-ready gate

`direct-delivery validate RUN` requires exactly:

- two selected pain-triggered targets;
- two selected artifact-first targets;
- three selected operators;
- seven evidence items;
- seven approval-queue items;
- all corresponding artifacts;
- no send action.

Use `--allow-incomplete` only for work-in-progress inspection.
